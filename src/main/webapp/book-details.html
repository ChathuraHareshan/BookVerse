The Quiet Cartographer

by Lena Whitfield · Published 2022 · 342 pages

4.2 · 186 member ratings

A retired mapmaker returns to the coastal town she once surveyed, only to find the shoreline — and her own memory of it — has quietly redrawn itself. Told across three monsoon seasons, The Quiet Cartographer follows Imogen Vale as she reconciles the maps she made with the town's living, shifting truth.

Whitfield's prose is patient and exact, treating memory the way her protagonist treats terrain: something that can be measured, but never fully fixed.
